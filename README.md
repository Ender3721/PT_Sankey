# PT_Sankey

To address the issues of implicit logical chains and broken data lineage 
among "raw measurement—mathematical processing—derived indicators" in 
traditional experimental records, this paper proposes a general-purpose 
variable data flow paradigm for building physics and multi-material 
experiments.<br>
Based on the Sankey diagram, an innovative variant termed 
**Physics-trace-sankey (PT_Sankey)** is developed to enable traceable 
visualization of physical quantities throughout the entire process.

---

为克服传统实验记录中"原始测量—数学处理—衍生指标"之间逻辑链条隐含、
数据血缘断裂的问题，本文提出一种面向建筑物理及多材料实验的变量数据流
通用范式，并基于桑基图创新出 PT_Sankey 变种形式，实现全过程物理量的
追溯可视化。

---
