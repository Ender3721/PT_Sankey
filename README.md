#简介#
为克服传统实验记录中“原始测量—数学处理—衍生指标”之间逻辑链条隐含、数据血缘（data lineage）断裂的问题，本文提出一种面向建筑物理及多材料实验的变量数据流通用范式，并基于桑基图（Sankey diagram），创新出一种变种形式Physics-trace-sankey（PT_Sankey），从而实现全过程物理量的追溯可视化。该范式以“实验目的—场景—仪器—原始数据—多级衍生数据”为骨架，将稳态统计判据与物理公式显式编码为结构化中间层，实现从测量到衍生指标的全链路可视化与机器可读解析，适用于多材料实验的通用数据血缘管理。

To address the issues of implicit logical chains and broken data lineage among "raw measurement—mathematical processing—derived indicators" in traditional experimental records, this paper proposes a general-purpose variable data flow paradigm for building physics and multi-material experiments. Based on the Sankey diagram, an innovative variant termed Physics-trace-sankey (PT_Sankey) is developed to enable traceable visualization of physical quantities throughout the entire process. The paradigm is structured around the backbone of "experimental objective—scenario—instrument—raw data—multi-level derived data," and explicitly encodes steady-state statistical criteria and physical formulas into a structured intermediate layer. This achieves end-to-end visualization and machine-readable parsing from measurements to derived indicators, making it applicable to general data lineage management for multi-material experiments.
